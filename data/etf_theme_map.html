How to read this
  • Universe. US-listed ETFs that are at least 3 years old and trade at least $1M a day, excluding leveraged, inverse, bond, income and option-overlay funds. Themes come from keywords in the fund name; a theme needs at least 3 ETFs. Broad market, country and style funds are left out. The universe is rebuilt monthly; is the last rebuild.
  • Returns use adjusted prices (distributions included) converted to rupees, month-end bars, the latest bar being the current price. The theme line chains the equal-weight average of the ETFs that existed in each month.
  • Survivorship. Only funds that still trade are listed, so history looks slightly better than what an investor would have lived through.
  • Costs not included: rupee conversion and remittance charges, brokerage, and tax. Overlap between ETFs in a theme is high, so a theme is closer to one bet than several.
  • Information only, not investment advice.
