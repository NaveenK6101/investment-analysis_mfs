By Sep 2026, CDMO looks like it beat Nifty 50 by 6.2x — but only 4.6x against Nifty Smallcap 250. Roughly a quarter of the "outperformance" against Nifty 50 is just the small-cap segment running hot generally, not CDMO specifically. Match the benchmark to your question: Nifty 50 / 500 for "did this theme beat the market," a size-matched index (Smallcap 250, Midcap 150) for "did it beat other stocks its own size," the sector index for "did it beat its own sector" — that's the CDMO-vs-Pharma chart above.

Method: monthly closing prices, National Stock Exchange, via Yahoo Finance. Price return only — dividends aren't added back, so dividend-heavy names understate total return. Basket = simple average of the two stocks' rebased lines, recomputed independently each month.
