Which asset class is leading, and which is fading

Nifty 100, Nifty Midcap 150, Nifty Smallcap 250, Nifty 500 and Sensex show rotation WITHIN equity; Debt (six large liquid funds), Gold, Silver and Crypto (equal-weight BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP) are genuine alternative asset classes — all nine compared to Nifty 50, which is the reference, not a row: if everything sits in Lagging, plain large-cap equity is winning outright. The x-axis is how strong that trend is right now; the y-axis is whether that strength is accelerating or fading. Methodology: RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum are an open, transparent approximation of the JdK Relative Rotation Graph methodology (the exact StockCharts/Bloomberg formula is proprietary) — a 10-week rolling z-score of relative strength, then a second 10-week rolling z-score of that. Both are centered on 100: above 100 means stronger/accelerating relative to the asset class's own recent trend, not to zero. This is a read on trend, not a price target.

Each row runs from its own start (Nifty 100/500, Sensex, Gold and Silver from 2011; Nifty Midcap 150 from 2019, when that index itself began; Debt from 2013; Crypto from 2020-04 when Solana begins); relative strength is a ratio to Nifty 50, so no common start is needed. This is a level above the Sector Rotation Map and the main leaderboard's other categories: it answers "where is money flowing right now," both within equity and across asset classes, not "which fund or sector within one." AUM, TER and portfolio-overlap data are not available from any free, reliable source for this project's funds, so they aren't shown anywhere on this page.
