Which sectors are leading, and which are fading

Each sector is a composite of its 3 longest-tenured funds, compared to Nifty 500. The x-axis is how strong that trend is right now; the y-axis is whether that strength is accelerating or fading. The trail shows each sector's path over the last several weeks — where it's been tells you as much as where it is.

Methodology: RS-Ratio and RS-Momentum are an open, transparent approximation of the JdK Relative Rotation Graph methodology (the exact StockCharts/Bloomberg formula is proprietary) — a 10-week rolling z-score of relative strength, then a second 10-week rolling z-score of that. Both are centered on 100: above 100 means stronger/accelerating relative to the sector's own recent trend, not to zero. This is a read on trend, not a price target.

Composite start dates vary by sector — Manufacturing, Energy & Power, and PSU are recently-popular fund categories with limited fund history, so their trails are shorter and noisier than Banking, Pharma, Tech, Infrastructure, or FMCG.
